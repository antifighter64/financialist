              Frequently asked questions

              What is a CD?

              A certificate of deposit: you lock money away for a fixed term (3 months to 5 years) in exchange for a guaranteed interest rate, usually higher than savings accounts.

              What happens if I withdraw early?

              You'll typically forfeit 3–6 months of interest. Only put money in a CD you won't need before maturity.

              Are CDs better than high-yield savings?

              CDs win when you want a guaranteed rate and won't touch the money. Savings win for flexibility. Many people use both — a CD ladder plus an HYSA for emergencies.

              What is a CD ladder?

              Splitting money across CDs with staggered maturities (e.g., 1-, 2-, 3-year). As each matures you reinvest at current rates — smoothing out rate changes while keeping regular access.

                          Frequently asked questions

                          What is a high-yield savings account?

                          A savings account — usually from an online bank — paying far above the national average rate, typically with no monthly fees and FDIC insurance.

                          Are online banks safe?

                          Yes, if FDIC-insured. Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per depositor, per bank — same as a branch bank.

                          How often do savings rates change?

                          Banks adjust APYs when the Fed moves rates, sometimes monthly. The accounts we list have consistently stayed near the top.

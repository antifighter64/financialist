How the scores work

Read the full method on How we rank. Each category above states its own rubric, sources and last update date.

Where a ranking page shows a published rating, the score here is the same one. For every other category the score reflects each company's position in that category's ranking (first place 4.9, then 4.8, 4.7 and so on). Those rankings use the sources named on each full ranking page, such as JD Power 2026 results for auto insurance, published APYs for savings and posted expense ratios for index funds. Scores are not a guarantee of what you will be offered; your rate and approval depend on your own profile.

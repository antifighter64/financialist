Frequently asked questions

How much is car insurance per month?

The US average is roughly $150–$200/month for full coverage, but it ranges from under $80 to over $300 depending on state, age, and driving record.

How can I lower my car insurance?

Compare 3+ quotes every year, bundle home + auto, raise your deductible, ask about telematics/good-driver discounts, and keep your credit clean where states allow its use.

Is minimum coverage enough?

It's legal, but one serious accident can blow past state minimums and put your assets at risk. Most advisors suggest 100/300/100 liability as a floor.

Does my credit affect my insurance rate?

In most states, yes — insurers use credit-based insurance scores. California, Hawaii, Massachusetts, and Michigan restrict or ban the practice.

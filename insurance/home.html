        Frequently asked questions

        How much home insurance do I need?

        Enough to rebuild your home at current construction costs (not market value) plus liability coverage — usually $300k+. Get a replacement-cost estimate, not just your purchase price.

        Does home insurance cover floods?

        No — standard policies exclude flood. You need separate NFIP or private flood insurance, especially in flood zones.

        How can I lower my home insurance?

        Bundle with auto (often 15–25% off), raise your deductible, install security systems and storm shutters, and re-shop every 2 years.

Frequently asked questions

How do you make money with domains?

Buy undervalued names and resell them (flipping), lease them, or earn parking/ad revenue while you hold. Premium one-word .coms have appreciated for decades.

What is a domain marketplace?

A platform where sellers list domains and buyers browse or bid — Afternic, Sedo, and Atom are the biggest. Registrars like Dynadot and GoDaddy also run marketplaces.

What fees do marketplaces charge?

Typically 10–20% commission on a sale, plus optional listing or membership fees. Always check the fee schedule before listing.

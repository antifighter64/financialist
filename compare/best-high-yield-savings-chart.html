How Financialist rates savings accounts

Every account gets a score from 0 to 5. Our analysts weigh what matters most to savers:

  • APY (40%) — the rate you actually earn, checked against current published rates.
  • Fees & minimums (30%) — $0 monthly fees and $0 minimums score highest.
  • Access & experience (20%) — mobile app quality, transfers, ATM access, customer service.
  • Extras (10%) — sign-up bonuses, buckets/goals tools, paired checking.

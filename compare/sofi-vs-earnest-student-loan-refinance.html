SoFi: pros and cons

Pros
  • Clear rate table by term and type
  • No application, origination or prepayment fees
  • Terms from 5 to 20 years
Cons
  • Best published rates are for strong credit profiles
  • Refinancing federal loans gives up federal protections

Earnest: pros and cons

Pros
  • No origination fees
  • Pick the exact payment you want to pay
  • Skip-a-payment feature and grace period
Cons
  • Variable rates unavailable in several states
  • Florida borrowers pay a state stamp tax
  • Refinancing federal loans gives up federal protections

Our take

Check both. Earnest fits borrowers who want to set an exact payment; SoFi fits borrowers who want to compare a published rate table across terms. Both use a soft credit check to show your rate. If your loans are federal, think hard before refinancing: you give up income-driven repayment and forgiveness for good. Compare the APR and total cost, not the monthly payment.

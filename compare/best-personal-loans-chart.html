How Financialist rates personal loans

Every lender gets a score from 0 to 5. Our analysts weigh what matters most to borrowers:

  • Cost (40%) — starting APR and origination fees; no-fee lenders score higher.
  • Access (25%) — minimum credit score and income requirements; wider access scores higher.
  • Loan flexibility (20%) — amount range, term options, and repayment flexibility.
  • Speed & experience (15%) — funding time, application ease, customer service.

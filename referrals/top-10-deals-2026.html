10 Refer-a-Friend Deals That Pay You Real Money in 2026

Referral bonuses are free money. You invite a friend. They sign up. You both get paid.

Referral bonuses are free money.
You invite a friend.
They sign up.
You both get paid.

Here are the 10 best deals paying real cash and stock in 2026.

🏆 1. Robinhood — Up to $15,000 in Free Stock

The biggest referral program on this list.
By far.

Invite 1 friend = $50 in free stock.
3 friends = $150.
10 friends = $800.
50 friends = $4,000.
200 friends = $10,000.

Max it out and earn up to $15,000 per year.

Your friend only opens an account and deposits any amount.
Even $1 works.
They also get free stock worth $5–$200.

No trading experience needed.
This is the deal to share first.

⭐ Rating: ★★★★★ — the highest-paying referral program anywhere.

💸 2. BetMGM — $100 Per Referral

BetMGM pays you $100 for every friend who joins and bets.

You can refer up to 20 friends per month.
That is up to $2,000 a month in potential bonuses.

The biggest per-friend payout on this list.
Perfect if your friends like sports betting.

⭐ Rating: ★★★★☆ — huge per-friend payout, but friends must actually bet.

💸 3. DraftKings — $100 Per Referral

DraftKings matches BetMGM's $100 payout.

You get 5 referrals per offer window.
That is up to $500 per window.

Another strong pick for sports fans.

⭐ Rating: ★★★★☆ — great payout, smaller referral cap than BetMGM.

💸 4. Fanatics — $100 in FanCash Per Referral

Fanatics pays $100 in FanCash for each friend.

5 referrals per window.
Up to $500 in FanCash.

Spend it on jerseys, gear, and sports merchandise.
Great if your friends are sports fans who shop.

⭐ Rating: ★★★★☆ — excellent value if you shop at Fanatics.

📈 5. Kalshi — $25 Per Referral, Up to $1,000

Kalshi is a prediction market.
Your friend signs up and trades $25.

You get $25 in bonus credits.
Up to 40 friends = $1,000 max.

Two things to know.
The bonus credits expire in 7 days.
And the bonus itself cannot be withdrawn — only your profits.

Not available in AZ, IL, MD, MI, MT, NJ, OH, or NV.

⭐ Rating: ★★★★☆ — easy money, but the credit expiry is tight.

🏈 6. FanDuel — $50 Per Referral

FanDuel pays $50 per friend.

Up to 5 referrals per 30 days.
That is $250 a month.

Lower than DraftKings and BetMGM.
Still solid free money.

⭐ Rating: ★★★☆☆ — half the payout of its rivals.

🎰 7. Caesars — $50 Per Referral

Caesars pays $50 per friend.

You can refer up to 10 per month.
That is $500 a month in bonuses.

⭐ Rating: ★★★☆☆ — decent monthly cap, average payout.

🎰 8. Hard Rock Bet — $50 Per Referral

Hard Rock Bet pays $50 per friend.

20 referrals per month.
That is up to $1,000 a month.

The highest monthly cap in the $50 tier.

⭐ Rating: ★★★☆☆ — great cap for a $50 deal.

📊 9. Polymarket — $10 Per Referral

Polymarket pays $10 for each friend who signs up.

No trading match needed.
No cap hassles.
Simple and instant.

Smallest payout here.
But the easiest to earn.

⭐ Rating: ★★★☆☆ — small but effortless.

🪙 10. Crypto Referral Bonuses — $10 to $50 Per Friend

Crypto apps pay you to bring friends.

Coinbase: $10 in Bitcoin each. Your friend makes one $100+ trade.
Kraken: random $5–$50 each. Up to $1,000 total. Friend deposits and trades.
Crypto.com: $25 in CRO each.
Gemini: $10 in Bitcoin each. Friend trades $100 within 30 days.
Binance: up to 50% of your friend's trading fees as ongoing commission.

If your friends are into crypto, stack these.

⭐ Rating: ★★★☆☆ — good bonuses, friends must trade first.

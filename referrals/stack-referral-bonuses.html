How to Stack Referral Bonuses Into Real Side Income

One referral bonus is nice.

By Financialist · October 17, 2026

Disclosure: This article contains referral links. If you sign up through them, we may earn a bonus at no extra cost to you.

One referral bonus is nice.

Ten referral bonuses stacked together is a side income.

The trick is simple.

Sign up for every program.

Then share your links in the right order.

This guide shows you exactly how to do it — and which programs to hit first.

🧠 The Big Idea in One Sentence

Every app on your phone wants your friends.

Most of them will pay you real money to bring them.

Stack enough of these payouts and you are earning hundreds or thousands of dollars a month.

No selling.

No skills needed.

Just links and friends.

If you only do one thing in this article, do this.

Robinhood has the best referral program of any app we have seen.

It is tiered, so it rewards you more as you share more:

  • 1 friend = $50 in free stock
  • 3 friends = $150
  • 10 friends = $800
  • 50 friends = $4,000
  • 200 friends = $10,000

Total possible: $15,000 per year.

Your friend only has to open an account and deposit any amount.

Even $1.

They get free stock too — worth $5 to $200.

This is the easiest ask in the entire referral world.

"Put in a dollar, get free stock."

Everyone says yes.

Get your Robinhood link first.

It is the engine of your whole stacking strategy.

🧮 Step 3: The Math That Makes It Real

Let us run some real numbers.

Say you share your links with friends and family over a few weeks.

Robinhood: 10 friends = $800

BetMGM: 20 friends = $2,000

Caesars: 10 friends = $500

Kalshi: 10 friends = $250

Polymarket: 10 friends = $100

Total: $3,650

That is one round of sharing.

Most of these caps reset every month.

So next month, you can do it again.

📬 Step 4: Share in the Right Order

The order matters.

Start with the easiest asks.

Finish with the hardest.

Round 1 — the easy yeses.

Share Robinhood and Polymarket first.

$1 deposit.

No trading needed.

Almost nobody says no to these.

Round 2 — the money apps.

Share Coinbase, Gemini, Kraken, and Crypto.com.

People are curious about crypto.

$10–$25 in free crypto is an easy pitch.

Round 3 — the betting apps.

Share BetMGM, DraftKings, Fanatics, FanDuel, Caesars, and Hard Rock Bet.

Only share these with friends who actually bet.

Never push gambling on someone who does not want it.

Round 4 — the long game.

Share Binance last.

It pays slow, but it pays forever.

⚠️ Step 5: The Rules of the Game

A few things to keep you out of trouble.

One link per message.

Do not blast all your links at once.

People tune out.

Track what you share.

Write down who got which link.

It takes two minutes and stops you from double-sending.

Watch the caps.

BetMGM cuts you off at 20 a month.

FanDuel resets every 30 days.

Know your limits before you share.

Check the fine print.

Kalshi has state restrictions.

Betting apps have playthrough rules.

Read the terms on every program.

Never spam strangers.

Your friends, your family, your group chats.

That is your audience.

Random spam gets you banned.

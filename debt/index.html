Federal rules that apply everywhere

Collectors can't sue on expired debt

Under the federal debt collection rule (Regulation F), a debt collector must not bring or threaten to bring a lawsuit to collect a time-barred debt, meaning a debt whose statute of limitations has run out.

12 CFR § 1006.26(b) · read the rule

Federal cap on wage garnishment

For ordinary debts, federal law caps garnishment at the lesser of 25% of disposable weekly earnings or the amount above 30 times the federal minimum wage. States can protect more, and many do.

15 U.S.C. § 1673 · read the law

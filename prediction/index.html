Frequently asked questions

What is a prediction market?

A platform where you buy and sell contracts tied to real-world events. If your prediction is right, the contract pays out — usually $1 per share.

Is Kalshi legal in the US?

Yes. Kalshi is a federally regulated exchange overseen by the CFTC, unlike offshore prediction sites.

Can you actually make money on prediction markets?

Skilled traders can, but most participants lose money — treat it as speculation, not investing.

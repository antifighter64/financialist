Frequently asked questions

How to compare loans?

APR first, then fees, then term. Lowest APR usually wins.

Soft vs hard pull?

Prequalification = soft (no impact). Formal application = hard pull.

How fast?

Online lenders fund in 1-3 days; banks take longer.

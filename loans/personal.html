                                                        Frequently asked questions

                                                        What credit score do I need for a personal loan?

                                                        Good rates start around 670. Fair-credit lenders approve down to ~580, but APRs run much higher. Below 580, consider a secured loan or credit-builder first.

                                                        How fast can I get a personal loan?

                                                        Online lenders fund as fast as same-day to 2 business days. Banks and credit unions take longer — up to a week.

                                                        Is a personal loan better than a credit card?

                                                        For large planned expenses, usually yes — installment APRs are far lower than credit card APRs, and fixed payments force payoff discipline.

                                                        Does checking my rate hurt my credit?

                                                        No — prequalification uses a soft inquiry. Only a formal application triggers a hard pull.

Frequently asked questions

Should I roll over?

Usually yes — IRAs have lower fees and more choices than most 401(k)s.

Direct vs indirect?

Direct (trustee-to-trustee) avoids withholding and deadlines.

What about my match?

Always contribute enough to get the full employer match first.

        Frequently asked questions

        How much money do I need to start investing?

        $0 at most major brokerages now — fractional shares let you buy $1 of any stock or ETF.

        Should I use a brokerage or a robo-advisor?

        A brokerage if you want to pick investments yourself; a robo-advisor if you want automation. Many people use both.

        What is an IRA match?

        Some brokers (like Robinhood) add 1–3% of your IRA contributions as a bonus. It's free money on retirement savings — rare and valuable.

        Are my investments safe if the brokerage fails?

        Yes — SIPC insurance covers up to $500,000 in securities ($250,000 cash) per customer if a brokerage goes under.

Frequently asked questions

Who qualifies?

Veterans, active-duty, Guard/Reserve, some surviving spouses. You need a COE.

Down payment?

None required — 0% down with no PMI.

VA funding fee?

One-time fee (~2.15% first use), can be rolled in. Disabled veterans exempt.

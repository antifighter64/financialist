Best Travel Credit Cards — October 2026

Travel cards turn your everyday spending into free trips. Welcome bonuses alone can be worth $750 or more in travel. Offers below are as of October 2026 — bonuses change often, so check the issuer's site.

Travel cards turn your everyday spending into free trips. Welcome bonuses alone can be worth $750 or more in travel. Offers below are as of October 2026 — bonuses change often, so check the issuer's site.

1. Chase Sapphire Preferred® Card — 4.8/5

Best for: First travel card

  • Welcome offer: 75,000 bonus points after $5,000 in spending in the first 3 months
  • Annual fee: $95
  • Earning: 5x on travel through Chase Travel, 3x on dining, streaming, online groceries, gas, and EV charging, 2x on other travel, 1x everywhere else
  • Perks: $100 annual hotel credit through Chase Travel, $120 Global Entry/TSA PreCheck credit every 4 years

Pros

  • Huge bonus worth $750+ in travel
  • Points transfer 1:1 to airline and hotel partners
  • Strong everyday bonus categories

Cons

  • $5,000 spend requirement is steep for some
  • $95 annual fee

2. Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card — 4.7/5

Best for: Simple flat-rate travel miles

  • Welcome offer: 75,000 miles after $4,000 in spending in the first 3 months, plus a $300 travel credit in year one
  • Annual fee: $95
  • Earning: 2x miles on every purchase; 5x on hotels, vacation rentals, and rental cars through Capital One Travel
  • Perks: Up to $120 Global Entry/TSA PreCheck credit, no foreign transaction fees

Pros

  • 2x on everything — no categories to think about
  • Miles work as simple statement credits for any travel
  • $300 first-year travel credit offsets the fee several times over

Cons

  • Travel credit only works through Capital One Travel
  • Transfer partners are weaker than Chase's

3. Capital One Venture X Rewards Credit Card — 4.6/5

Best for: Premium travel perks

  • Welcome offer: 75,000 miles after $4,000 in spending in the first 3 months
  • Annual fee: $395
  • Earning: 10x on hotels and rental cars through Capital One Travel, 5x on flights through Capital One Travel, 2x on everything else
  • Perks: Airport lounge access, $300 annual travel credit, 10,000 bonus miles each anniversary

Pros

  • Lounge access at a lower fee than competing premium cards
  • Annual credits nearly offset the $395 fee
  • Strong earning on all travel booked through the portal

Cons

  • High annual fee if you don't travel much
  • Best value requires booking through Capital One Travel

How Financialist rates life insurers

Every provider gets a score from 0 to 5. Our analysts weigh what matters most to real policyholders:

  • Financial strength (30%) — AM Best ratings and the insurer's ability to pay claims decades from now.
  • Policy variety (25%) — term, whole, universal, and indexed options, plus riders (accelerated death benefit, waiver of premium, child term).
  • Pricing value (25%) — competitiveness of premiums and policy fees relative to coverage.
  • Application experience (20%) — speed, medical-exam requirements, digital underwriting, and agent support.

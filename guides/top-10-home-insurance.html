How Financialist rates home insurers

Every insurer gets a score from 0 to 5. Our analysts weigh what matters most to real homeowners:

  • Coverage quality (30%) — dwelling, personal property, liability, loss of use, plus valuable add-ons (extended replacement cost, water backup, equipment breakdown, ordinance coverage).
  • Pricing & discounts (25%) — competitiveness of base rates and discount depth (bundling, protective devices, claims-free, new home).
  • Financial strength & claims (25%) — AM Best financial-strength ratings, claims satisfaction, and complaint records.
  • Digital experience (20%) — quoting, policy management, and claims filing online and in-app.

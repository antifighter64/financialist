How Financialist rates cash back credit cards

Every card gets a score from 0 to 5. Our analysts weigh what matters most to real cardholders:

  • Rewards value (35%) — how much the average spender actually earns back in a year, after caps and categories.
  • Fees (25%) — annual fee weighed against what you get for it; $0-fee cards score higher here.
  • Intro offers (20%) — welcome bonuses and 0% intro APR periods.
  • Perks & protections (20%) — purchase protections, cell-phone coverage, travel perks, and similar.

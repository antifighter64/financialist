10 Best Small Business Loans of October 2026

Banks say no to most small-business borrowers. Online lenders say yes in hours — at a price. We ranked the top lenders on speed, amounts, qualification requirements, and true cost so you can fund growth without overpaying.

Compare the true cost, not just the monthly payment. Many online lenders quote a factor rate (e.g. 1.2) instead of an APR — a 1.2 factor on a 6-month loan can equal a triple-digit APR. Always convert quotes to an annualized rate before comparing, watch for daily or weekly repayment schedules, and never borrow more than your cash flow can service.

Amounts, credit minimums, and funding speeds shown as of October 2026 and vary by applicant — always confirm current terms with the lender.

How Financialist rates business lenders

Every lender gets a score from 0 to 5. Our analysts weigh what matters most to borrowers:

  • Cost of capital (30%) — true APRs and total repayment cost vs. amount borrowed.
  • Speed & ease (25%) — application simplicity and time to funding.
  • Qualification accessibility (25%) — credit, revenue, and time-in-business minimums.
  • Product fit (20%) — loan types, amounts, terms, and repayment flexibility.

How Financialist rates high-yield savings accounts

Every account gets a score from 0 to 5. Our analysts weigh:

  • APY and rate consistency (40%) — the headline rate and how reliably the bank keeps it near the top of the market.
  • Fees and minimums (25%) — monthly fees, balance minimums, and hoops to earn the advertised APY.
  • Access and tools (20%) — mobile app quality, ATM access, transfers, and savings tools like buckets.
  • Bonuses (15%) — welcome offers and ongoing promotions.

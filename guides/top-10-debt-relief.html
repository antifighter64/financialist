10 Best Debt Relief Companies of October 2026

Drowning in credit card debt? The right debt relief company can negotiate your balances down — but the wrong one can make things worse. We ranked the top firms on track record, transparency, and client outcomes so you can get help with confidence.

Debt relief isn't right for everyone. Debt settlement typically requires you to stop paying creditors, which can damage your credit score, trigger collection calls, and leave forgiven debt taxable as income (IRS Form 1099-C). No company can guarantee results, and federal rules bar reputable firms from charging upfront fees before settling a debt. Consider nonprofit credit counseling (look for NFCC-member agencies), a bankruptcy attorney consultation, or a DIY payoff plan (avalanche/snowball) before enrolling in any settlement program.

How Financialist rates debt relief companies

Every company gets a score from 0 to 5. Our analysts weigh what matters most to people in debt:

  • Track record & scale (30%) — years in business, debts settled, and client volume.
  • Transparency (25%) — clear fee disclosure, honest timelines, no upfront-fee red flags.
  • Client experience (25%) — review patterns, dedicated support, and complaint history (BBB/CFPB).
  • Accessibility (20%) — minimum debt thresholds, state availability, and free consultations.

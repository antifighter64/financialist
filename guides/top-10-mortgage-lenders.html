How Financialist rates mortgage lenders

Every lender gets a score from 0 to 5. Our analysts weigh what matters most to real borrowers:

  • Rates & fees (35%) — how the lender's typical rates, origination fees, and closing costs compare to the market.
  • Loan variety (25%) — breadth of products: conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, jumbo, and non-QM options.
  • Customer experience (25%) — application ease, communication, on-time closing record, and verified borrower reviews.
  • Digital tools (15%) — online application, document upload, rate tracking, and preapproval speed.

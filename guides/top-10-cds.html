10 Best CD Rates of October 2026

CD rates have come down from their peaks — but the best online banks still pay 10x the national average. Lock in a guaranteed return with one of these top-yielding certificates of deposit, all FDIC or NCUA insured.

Know the trade-off before you lock up cash. CDs pay a guaranteed rate, but withdrawing early triggers a penalty (typically 90–365 days of interest). Only deposit money you won't need before the term ends, and keep your emergency fund in a liquid high-yield savings account instead.

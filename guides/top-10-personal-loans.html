How Financialist rates personal loan lenders

Every lender gets a score from 0 to 5. Our analysts weigh:

  • APR and total cost (40%) — starting rates and how wide the range runs for typical borrowers.
  • Fees (20%) — origination, late, and prepayment fees; no-fee lenders score higher.
  • Eligibility and speed (25%) — minimum credit score, soft-pull pre-qualification, and how fast money arrives.
  • Repayment flexibility (15%) — term options, autopay discounts, hardship programs, and direct-pay for debt consolidation.

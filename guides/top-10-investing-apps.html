How Financialist rates investing apps and brokers

Every broker gets a score from 0 to 5. Our analysts weigh:

  • Commissions and fees (30%) — stock/ETF/options pricing, account fees, and margin rates.
  • Platform and tools (30%) — mobile app quality, charting, fractional shares, and crypto access.
  • Account options (20%) — IRAs, 401(k) rollovers, custodial accounts, and cash management.
  • Research and support (20%) — screeners, analyst research, education, and customer service.

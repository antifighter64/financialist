Best Cash Back Credit Cards — October 2026

The best cash back card is the one that fits how you already spend. We picked cards with no games — strong flat rates, big bonus categories, and $0 annual fees where possible.

By Financialist · October 17, 2026

Last updated: October 9, 2026 · Fact checked ✓

The best cash back card is the one that fits how you already spend. We picked cards with no games — strong flat rates, big bonus categories, and $0 annual fees where possible.

1. Wells Fargo Active Cash® Card — 4.8/5

Best for: Simple flat-rate cash back

  • Rewards: Unlimited 2% cash back on every purchase
  • Annual fee: $0
  • Welcome offer: Cash rewards bonus (check current offer)
  • APR: See issuer site for current rates

Pros

  • 2% on everything — no categories to track
  • $0 annual fee
  • Cell phone protection when you pay your bill with the card
  • Intro APR period on purchases and balance transfers

Cons

  • You can earn more in bonus categories with other cards
  • Wells Fargo's app gets mixed reviews

2. Citi Double Cash® Card — 4.7/5

Best for: Set-it-and-forget-it earners

  • Rewards: Up to 2% back — 1% when you buy, 1% when you pay
  • Annual fee: $0
  • Welcome offer: $200 cash back (spend requirement applies — check current terms)
  • APR: 18.49%–28.74% variable

Pros

  • 2% effective rate with no annual fee
  • Simple — no rotating categories
  • 0% intro APR on balance transfers for 18 months

Cons

  • You must pay your bill to get the full 2%
  • No big bonus spending categories

Apply now

3. Chase Freedom Unlimited® — 4.7/5

Best for: Everyday spending with bonus categories

  • Rewards: 5% on travel through Chase Travel, 3% on dining and drugstores, 1.5% on everything else
  • Annual fee: $0
  • Welcome offer: $200 cash back (check current spend requirement)
  • APR: 18.24%–27.74% variable; 0% intro APR for 15 months on purchases and balance transfers

Pros

  • Strong bonus categories for a $0-fee card
  • Pairs with Sapphire cards to turn cash back into travel points
  • Long 0% intro APR period

Cons

  • Base rate of 1.5% is lower than flat 2% cards
  • 3% foreign transaction fee

Apply now

4. Blue Cash Preferred® Card from American Express — 4.5/5

Best for: Groceries and streaming

  • Rewards: 6% at U.S. supermarkets (on up to $6,000/year), 6% on select streaming, 3% on transit and gas
  • Annual fee: $150 (check current fee — it has changed before)
  • Welcome offer: Check current bonus offer

Pros

  • Highest grocery rewards rate of any major card
  • Strong streaming and gas categories too
  • Great if your biggest bills are food

Cons

  • Has an annual fee — do the math on your grocery spend
  • 6% grocery rate is capped each year

Apply now

5. Discover it® Cash Back — 4.5/5

Best for: Rotating bonus categories

  • Rewards: 5% in rotating quarterly categories (up to $1,500/quarter, activation required), 1% on everything else
  • Annual fee: $0
  • Welcome offer: Cashback Match — Discover matches all cash back earned in year one
  • APR: 17.74%–26.74% variable

Pros

  • First-year Cashback Match can double your rewards
  • No annual fee, no foreign transaction fee
  • Free FICO score

Cons

  • You must activate categories every quarter
  • 5% is capped; base 1% rate is low

Apply now

6. Capital One Savor Cash Rewards — 4.4/5

Best for: Dining and entertainment

  • Rewards: 3% on dining, entertainment, and streaming; 1% on everything else
  • Annual fee: $0
  • Welcome offer: Check current bonus offer

Pros

  • Best $0-fee card for dining out
  • Entertainment category covers tickets, concerts, and more
  • No foreign transaction fee

Cons

  • 1% base rate is weak for non-bonus spending
  • Grocery rewards are thin

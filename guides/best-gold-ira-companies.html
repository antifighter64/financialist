Best gold IRA companies: the fee questions behind the pitch

Augusta, Goldco and Birch Gold are worth comparing, but a gold IRA is not a free shelter from market risk. Start with the minimum, the all-in cost and the price at which you could sell tomorrow.

By James Park · Checked September 26, 2026

Fees: ask for the price to buy and the price to sell

All three connect the metal purchase to separate custody and storage. Goldco's FAQ estimates about $50 to set up an account, about $80 in annual account management and $100 to $150 in annual storage, while warning that choices vary. Birch Gold's example uses a $50 setup fee, $30 wire fee and $235 in annual custody and storage; its FAQ says a $5,000 minimum can change. Augusta lists setup, annual custodian, storage and insurance costs but does not establish a universal all-in price on its public guide.

The most important missing number is often the dealer's spread: the difference between what you pay and what the dealer would offer to buy the same item back. Ask all three for the same eligible coin or bar, weight, current spot price, purchase price, immediate buyback price, custodian/depository charges and any commission in writing. Do not treat a “free metals” promotion as free without comparing the metal price.

The regulator's warning matters even with familiar names

The CFTC and FINRA warn that physical precious metals can fluctuate and that some sellers overcharge retirees through markups and commissions. That warning is about the product and sales market, not an accusation against these three companies. Gold does not pay interest or dividends, and a concentrated rollover can sacrifice diversification. An independent adviser or tax professional can check whether a metal allocation belongs in your retirement plan.

IRS rules limit which metals qualify inside a retirement account. A dealer cannot turn an ineligible collectible into an IRA investment by calling it “IRA approved.” Confirm eligibility with the custodian before you pay. Mistakes in rollovers can create taxes or penalties.

A five-minute comparison checklist

  • Ask which custodian and depository will hold the account and get their current schedules.
  • Compare written buy and same-day buyback quotes for the same metal and weight.
  • Check the first-year and recurring fees at your actual account size.
  • Ask what happens if you need cash or must take a required distribution.
  • Check your overall mix of investments, not just the metal price.

Compare other retirement account options before you roll over. A gold IRA is one possible holding, not a replacement for a diversified retirement plan.

Before you roll over

  • A gold IRA is still an IRA - the same contribution, distribution and penalty rules apply. The metal changes, not the tax wrapper.
  • Ask for the complete fee schedule in writing: setup, annual admin, storage, and the spread on the metal itself. The spread is where costs hide.
  • IRS rules require an approved custodian and depository. Home storage of IRA metals is a taxable distribution - any company suggesting otherwise is a red flag.
  • Metals are a hedge, not a growth engine. Most fiduciaries cap commodity allocations at 5-10% of a portfolio.

FAQ

What does a gold IRA cost? Typically a one-time setup fee, $100-$300 in annual administration, and storage fees - plus the dealer spread on the metal, which varies the most.

Can I hold the gold at home? Not inside an IRA. IRS rules require an approved depository; home storage triggers taxes and penalties.

How do I fund one? Rollover or transfer from an existing IRA or 401(k), or a new annual contribution within IRA limits.

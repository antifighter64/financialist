10 Best Credit Monitoring Services of October 2026

Data breaches hit billions of records a year. A good credit monitoring service watches your credit files and the dark web around the clock — and helps you recover if your identity is stolen. We ranked the best free and paid options.

Free options cover the basics. You don't need to pay to watch your credit: federal law gives you free weekly credit reports at AnnualCreditReport.com, and several services on this list offer genuinely free monitoring. Paid plans add convenience — 3-bureau coverage, identity-theft insurance, and restoration help — but no service can prevent all identity theft. Consider a free credit freeze at all three bureaus for the strongest protection.

How Financialist rates credit monitoring services

Every service gets a score from 0 to 5. Our analysts weigh what matters most:

  • Identity-theft protection (25%) — insurance limits, restoration services, fraud-resolution support.
  • Value (25%) — features per dollar; free tiers score well here.

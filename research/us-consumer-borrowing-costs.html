U.S. Consumer Borrowing Costs, 2021 to 2026: Credit Card, Personal Loan and Auto Rates

Credit card interest sits near 21% on average. A 24-month personal loan averages 11.86%. Here are the Federal Reserve's numbers side by side.

Federal Reserve G.19 release of September 8, 2026 · Last updated October 5, 2026 · Download CSV
Key numbers (2026 Q2). Credit card, accounts assessed interest: 22.15%. Personal loan, 24-month: 11.86%. New car loan, 60-month at commercial banks: 7.14%. A card balance costs about 10 points more a year than a personal loan, on average. That gap is why debt consolidation can save money, but only if the new rate and term beat the old one for your credit profile.

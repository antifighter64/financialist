How to read this

Rates are the basic hourly minimum shown in the Department of Labor's state table, which is dated January 1, 2026. When a state has no law, or its rate is below the federal rate, covered employers must pay the federal $7.25. Some cities and counties set higher local minimums, and some states have different rates for tipped or small-employer workers; those are not shown. Rates can change during the year, so check your state labor department before relying on a figure.

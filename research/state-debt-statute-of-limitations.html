How many years a creditor or collector has to sue over an unpaid debt in each state, with the statute behind every number.

Last updated October 5, 2026 · 51 jurisdictions · Download CSV
Key point. The limit depends on the type of debt (written, oral, open account). Paying a little on an old debt can restart the clock in some states, and in some it cannot. Check the state page before you pay anything. This is general information, not legal advice.

How we collected this

Each figure comes from the state's own statute, linked on that state's Financialist page. We copied the figures from those pages, which were each checked against the linked law. Where a state has more than one limit we list each one. Where a state's link goes to a Justia mirror of the code, the mirror is labeled. The federal rule on suing over time-barred debt is in Regulation F (12 CFR 1006.26). Laws change; read the current text before acting.

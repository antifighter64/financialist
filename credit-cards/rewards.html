Frequently asked questions

Points or cash back?

Cash is simple and never devalues. Points win for frequent travelers using transfer partners.

How much are points worth?

Roughly 1-2 cents each, more with smart transfers. Never hoard — they devalue.

Do rewards expire?

Usually not while the account is open, but check your issuer.

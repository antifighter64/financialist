      Frequently asked questions

      What is a good cash back rate?

      2% flat is the benchmark for excellent. Bonus categories at 3–5% are great if they match your spending; otherwise a flat 2% card wins on simplicity.

      Are cash back cards better than points cards?

      For most people, yes — cash is flexible and never devalues. Points win only if you travel often and use transfer partners well.

      Do cash back rewards expire?

      Usually not, as long as the account stays open and in good standing. Check your issuer's terms.

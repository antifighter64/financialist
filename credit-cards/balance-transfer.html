Frequently asked questions

How does a balance transfer work?

You move debt from a high-APR card to a new card with a 0% intro period. You pay a one-time transfer fee (usually 3–5%), then pay no interest during the promo window.

Will a balance transfer hurt my credit?

Temporarily — a hard inquiry plus a new account. But lowering your utilization by spreading balances usually helps your score within months.

What happens when the 0% period ends?

Any remaining balance starts accruing interest at the card's regular APR. Have a payoff plan: divide the balance by the number of 0% months and autopay that amount.
